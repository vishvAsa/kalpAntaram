Many a scholars (from Hopkins to Lariviere) have held this position that the Mānava-Dharmaśāstra was formed through this agentless redaction by successive authors like some other H-texts. But the contrary view has existed since Bühler.

Olivelle also seems to suggest otherwise. Olivelle's list of interpolated verses:
1.32 - 57; 1.61 - 119; 2.88 - 100; 8.20 - 22; 8.27 - 40; 8.386 - 420; 9.229 - 49; 9.294 - 311; 9.313 - 23; 10.1 - 73 (?); 11.1 - 44; 11.127 - 79; 11.191 - 247 (?); 12.108 - 15; 12.117 - 26
