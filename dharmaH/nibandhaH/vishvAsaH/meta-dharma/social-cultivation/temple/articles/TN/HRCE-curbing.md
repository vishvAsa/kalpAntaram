## Trustees
The Madras High Court’s recent ruling in a PIL related to the Sri Parthasarathy Swamy Temple, Thiruvallikeni filed by our President Shri @trramesh
 marks a welcome legal development.

The case raises an important question of temple governance: can an Executive Officer/Fit Person, who is a Government servant, continue to run an ancient temple in the absence of a duly appointed Board of Trustees?

This is not a mere technicality. Trustees play a crucial role in protecting temple property, funds, rituals, customs, festivals, and devotee rights. When temples are run indefinitely through temporary administrative arrangements, accountability and transparency suffer.

Sri Parthasarathy Swamy Temple, Thiruvallikeni, Chennai, is one of the 108 Divya Desams. Its governance must be lawful, transparent, and rooted in the interests of the deity and devotees.
