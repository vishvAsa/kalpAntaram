## Former state
Years ago each sivan temple  in Thanjavur Jilla had a dedicated Kurukkal. They lived in Agraharam,took care of the presiding deity, did nithyapadi poojai, 4 kala poojai,6 kaala poojai,as the case may be.
Each Vaishnava temple had a dedicated Archagar. They followed the agamam,performed thirumanajanam on Thirunakshathram etc.,  Mahasamprokshanam,Samvathsaram were done as per the tradition.
Over the years,due to many reasons, the situation has changed.

## Current state
Today in and around Kumbakonam,Thanjavur side you have one Kurukkal servicing 6,7 or even 8 temples.
The impact is two ways.
1. In order to sustain a living the Kurukkal has to accept,serve all of those temples 
2. The Dharma Karthas have sought the support of Kurukkal to perform oru kaala poojai atleast.

Just think about 10 -12 years down the line.
