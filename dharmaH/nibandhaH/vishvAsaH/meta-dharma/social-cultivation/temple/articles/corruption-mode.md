## Renovations
Renovations are done primarily from the temple's deposit accumulated through donations and rent excess. Say a temple has 100 crores in their acct, temple will bill the renovation at 90 crores, give contract to a politician run company, who will do it substandard at 30 crores and bill the temple at 90. Give a share of 60 to the agama vyapari (who signs off and keeps quiet).

## Samprokshanas
Samprokshanas are always carried out just before elections. 

Over the past year probably a third of the major SV kshetras had their samprokshana. Local party outfits use temple funds to fund their election expenses and also use it for PR to get votes.
