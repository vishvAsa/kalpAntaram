Whether one wants to analyse it academically or theologically, I think the Vipras tried their best to integrate any and all friendly elements among the anārya janas as well as undo some of the more obsolete conceits relating to caturthas:


## Raise to kShatriyas
- JUB expressly speaks of how anāryas may become possessed of rājna (kṣātram?) through a sāmaveda-prayoga involving bha/bhā śabda. एतं ह वा एतं न्यङ्गमनु कुभ्र इति यद्भ इति निगच्छति तस्मात्सोऽनार्यस्सन्नपि राज्ञः प्राप्नोति ५ +++(4)+++
- Related – Manu’s lore that many ārya royalties were merely fallen Kṣatriyas, which has the implicit seed-idea that their Kṣātram may perhaps be revived. 
- The Mahābhārata’s foresightful prescription through the device of a Yudhiṣṭhira-Bhīṣma saṃvāda that when Kṣatriyas are no longer available, Śūdras  may take up the mantle of protecting Dharma. 
- Śaivāgama’s prescription that pious Sacchūdras who have taken up Dīkṣā and given up alcohol should be treated as equivalent to Kṣatriyas. +++(??)+++

Be it Śivāji or Prolaya Vema Reddy or Vanniyars/Nāḍārs receiving vaidika upanayana (both under Śaiva Vipras—former under Tillai Dīkṣitas, latter under Ādiśaivas)—or Kammas/Reddys receiving upanayana from certain Vipras, I don’t think any of these phenomena can be perceived in isolation from the above points.
