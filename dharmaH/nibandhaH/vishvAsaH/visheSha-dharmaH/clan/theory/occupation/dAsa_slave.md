## Volunatary dAsI-s
[TW](/AgamaH_shaivaH/meta/articles/schwartz-jason/shaiva-age-end/02_A_Beginningless_Transactionality) quoting Svayamāgata-dāsī-patra-vidhi in lekhana-paddhati.

> Seeking to spare the family one more mouth to feed, Sampūrī slipped away ...  amidst her begging, she took to going door to door beseeching ... "Will you take me as your slave?” (*deva! anāthāṃ pratigṛham iti vadantī ko ’pi dāsīṃ rahāpayatīti bhāṣamāṇā*). Time and again, people refused. .... Her offer was accepted... and the bill of sale classified as “A binding document regarding a willing woman selling herself into slavery,”.   


### Terms
> Sampūrī commits to carrying out the orders of all members of the family for the rest of her life. If, out of her own self-satisfaction, she refuses, ... “bind her, beat her and so forth” (*bandhanatāḍanādikaṃ*), while at the same time [[P308]] absolving her master of any guilt or fault.... The same holds true if she commits suicide or perishes in some similar manner while in his service.
> 
> She is not to steal, to run off with some man, to associate with rivals of the family, or to abandon her work and resume the life of a beggar. ... 
> 
> In exchange, the master’s only responsibility is to house his new slave and provide her clothes, food, and shoes, insomuch as he is able due to circumstance.
