> What did traditional marriage get right that the modern marriage will always miss?

Marriage was understood as something you entered and served, not something you continuously evaluated according to how well it served you. You didn't remain married because you continually felt in love. You learned how to love because you'd promised to remain married.+++(5)+++

Marriage can't endure if it's merely an agreement between two sovereign individuals to satisfy one another. It becomes durable when husband and wife understand themselves as custodians of a good greater than either of them.
