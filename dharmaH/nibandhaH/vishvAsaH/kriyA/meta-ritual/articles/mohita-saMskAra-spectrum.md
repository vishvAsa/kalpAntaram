## Levels
there’re two levels of anushthana,  
one where every minor act is also a ritual in itself, ex. milking the cow, checking the ghee for impurities, overflowing havishya from the ladle and falling on the grass/ground, the shrauta rituals are such. This is the topmost. The idea behind each act is to impress upon a Samskara on the chitta of not only the doer, but of the audience as well. 

The other end is where ritual part is kept at the minimum, as the target Samskara is very low level. But by the power of repetition, is expected to bring high impact on the chitta of the doer. 

## Impact difference
A person who rears his cow, cuts n collects his wood, lights his Agni, then performs his ritual w/ minimum participation of the world, will have much higher achievement, than a person who starts from the other end. 

The other end starting person is not a bad person, but he must keep in mind as to where he stands and how much distance he wishes to cover in this life. 

## Factors
It is for us to decide for ourselves where we fall in this spectrum. For that, like I mentioned many times earlier, Viveka >> punya >> satsangati >> bhagavat kRpa is the way.
