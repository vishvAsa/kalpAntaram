Chicken are offered to KA grAma devatAs representing jaya and vijaya ([TW](https://youtu.be/QywwytTyCPs?t=78) ). By extension eggs are allowed there.

> In certain esoteric tantric ways, dimba (egg) bali given.

Source: [TW](https://x.com/shardula23/status/2070913731647902152)

I guess in Bengal it is mostly uncommon to give Morog bali to Kali or deities worshipped though Smarta praxis like Durga, some exceptional temples may have. Some have the Kulachara to offer duck eggs to Manasā(I am not sure if they are cooked and offered or just placed on the asana). But they offer rooster sacrifice in Nepal. Also wild rooster is bhakshya in Smritis but Grāmakukkuta is abhakshya.
