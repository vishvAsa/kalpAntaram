Source: ravilochana

Asvalayana says atithi pitaro vivahashcha

But there is a point to be noted. Chhaaga pashu nyaya must be adopted here Aitareya says that only a barren cow or a castrated ox should be used for madhuparka.

Shatapatha has a statement that one should not eat meat of cow and bull. But that yajnavalkya was ok with eating calf meat. So I think originally it must have been that meat of the cow which gave us milk and meat of the bull which made the cow pregnant were avoided. 

Barren cow. Castrated ox and calves might have been ok when brahmanas still ate meat in the older era. 

But the rules progressed slowly wherein all cattle meat were banned first. Then we moved to full vegetarianism.
