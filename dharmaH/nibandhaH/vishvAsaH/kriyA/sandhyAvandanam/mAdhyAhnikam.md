> This act of adoration is sometimes prescribed as necessary thrice a day viz. at day-break, in the noon and at sun-set e.g. Atri[^741] says 'a twice born person possessed of the knowledge of the Self should perform three samdhyA adorations.'  … Generally however the sandhya prayer is prescribed twice ( Aśv. gṛ. III. 7, Ap. Dh. S. I. 11. 30. 8, Gaut. II. 17, Manu II. 101, Yaj. I. 24-25 &o).
