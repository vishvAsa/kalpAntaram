76. Pārijāta

Several works bear the appellation Pārijāta such as the Vidhānapārijāta (composed in 1625 A. D.), the [[Madanapāri. jāta|Madanapārijāta]] (about 1375 [[A. 1).|A. D.]]) and the Prayogapārijāta (between 1400-1500). But there was an ancient work called Pārijāta quoted by even some of the earliest *[[nibuni hakāries|nibandhakāras]]*. The Vivādaratnākara regarded the [[Kaljataru|Kalpataru]], [[Parijata Halā yudha|Pārijāta Halāyudha]] and Prakāśa as its most eminent predecessors.[^893] The Dānaratnākara[^894] quotes several times the views of Pārijāta on gifts. The Smṛtisāra of Harinātha sets out at length [[tbe|the]] order of succession to a sonless [[inan|man]] according to the Pārijāta (vide [[I, O.|I. O.]] Ms. No. 301, folio 134 a). One of the striking opinions of the Pārijāta was that the widow of a sonless man should raise offspring by *niyoga* and give the wealth of the deceased to the son so born.

[^893]:
    कल्पद्रुमे वाप्यथ पारिजाते हलायुधे वाप्यथवा प्रकाशे । मत्सारमम्भादधिकं च यनदब्धनाति रत्नाकर एक एव ॥ *verse at the end of* वि. र.

[^894]:
    ऋत्विग्भ्यस्तुं दक्षिणामात्रम् । भूपालसागरपारिजातादयोप्येवम् । दानरत्नाकर folio 28 a ; अथवा तुलापुरुषद्वदर्थं गुरवे निवेद्यार्धमृत्विग्भ्यो दद्यादिति पारिजातः । *ibid* folio 28 b ( about कामधेनुप्रदान ).

From the above it appears that the Pārijāta was an [[in dependent|independent]] work on *[[16'hora, dūnu|vyavahāra, dāna]]* and other topics, as it held [[vieirs|views]] similar to those of Bhojadeva and [[Halāyudba|Halāyudha]] on the widow's rights and as it is not quoted by the [[Mit,|Mit.]] or other earlier works it must be assigned to a date between 1000-1125 A. D. In I. L. R. 12 Cal. 348 at p. 356 the [[learneri|learned]] judges hold that the Pārijāta frequently [[citel|cited]] as an authority by the Vivādaratnākara is the Madanapārijāta. But this is entirely wrong. The latter work, it will be shown (*[[viile|vide]]* sec. [[P656]] [[Madana pārijāta|Madanapārijāta]] ) was composed about 1375, while the Vivādaratnākara was composed about 1320 A. D. The Kṛtyaratnākara [[meutions|mentions]] the Pārijāta [[pine times|nine times]] (on pp. 31, 54, 58, 59, 61, 66, 74, 92, 606) and Vivādaratnākara quotes it six times. The Pārijāta was liberal enough to state that the Pāñcarātra, Pāśupata and similar doctrines, where not opposed to the Veda, are authoritative as to those parts (Kṛtyaratnākara p. 31).

Raghunandana in Dāyatattva (vol. II. p. 177) [[Jumps|lumps]] together '[[Smrti-mahārnava|Smṛti-mahārnava]], [[Kamadhenu|Kāmadhenu]] and Pārijāta' and again on p. 195 of the same puts together the [[Mitāksarā|Mitākṣarā]], [[Pāri. jāta|Pārijāta]], [[Halāyudba|Halāyudha]] and holds that a certain passage in the Kalpataru (that differs) is due to a mistake of the copyist (vide [[foot--notesng|foot-note]][^895] below).

[^895]:
    रत्नाकरप्रभृतयस्तु-यस्तु कल्पतरौ नान्योदर्यं धनं हरेदिति पाठो दृश्यते स मूलभूतयाज्ञवल्क्यमिताक्षरापारिजातहलायुधग्रन्थेषु ‘नान्योदर्यो धनं हरेत्’-इति पाठदर्शनात्तदनुसारव्याख्यादर्शनाच्च लिपिकरप्रमाद इत्याहुः । दायतत्त्व ( II p. 155 ).
