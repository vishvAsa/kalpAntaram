40. Ṛṣyaśṛṅga

This is a writer who is frequently quoted on ācāra, āśauca, śrāddha, and [[prāyaścittu|prāyaścitta]] by the Mit., Aparārka, [[Smṛticand rikā|Smṛticandrikā]] and other works. [[A parārka|Aparārka]] (p. 724) quotes as [[Rsyas roga's|Ṛṣyaśṛṅga's]] a verse ascribed to [[Sarkha|Śaṅkha]] in the Mitākṣarā (on Yāj. II. 119) and other works, which states that when one [[copar cener|coparcener]] recovers with his own efforts family property that was lost to the family, he gets a fourth share of it and the others become sharers in the rest.[^584] The Smṛticandrikā (I. p. 32) quotes 'api vāsasi yajñopavītārthān kuryāt tadabhāve [[trivstā|trivṛtā]] sūtreṇa', which is in prose.[^584a] 

Ṛṣyaśṛṅga is frequently quoted by Aparārka on the Prāyaścitta section of Yāj. (about 13 verses) on Prāyaścitta. The Vy. N. of Varadarāja (p. 26) ascribes the verse of Yāj. II. 32 (Mattonmattārta &c) to [[Rsyaśṣnga|Ṛṣyaśṛṅga]] also. Aparārka (p. 724) quotes one verse of [[Rsya',|Ṛṣya°.]] on Vyavahāra viz. 'If one coparcener in a joint family recovers by his efforts [[pro perty|property]] lost to the [[fumily|family]], he should be awarded one-fourth of it and the remaining portion should be distributed among the remaining coparceners according to their proper shares. This verse is ascribed to [[Saikha|Śaṅkha]] by the Smṛticandrikā (on p. 276). Kalpataru (on Vy. 622) quotes two verses on a wife's duties.
