24 Paiṭhīnasi

What's in this page?
🐎💪 32%

Though not enumerated in [[Yājsavalkya|Yājñavalkya]], Paiṭhīnasi seems to have been a comparatively ancient sūtrakāra on Dharma.

On Yāj. III. 262 Viśvarūpa quotes a sūtra of Paiṭhīnasi on the prāyaścitta for kiling a cow. Dr. Jolly (R. und [[$|S]]. p. 12) following Dr. Caland [[("|(’]]Ahnencult &c.’ pp. 99, 109) thinks that Paiṭhīnasi [[belenys|belongs]] to the Atharvaveda as the prose quotations on [[frāddha|śrāddha]] agree with the ritual of the Atharvans. The Mit. (on Yāj. I. 53) quotes a sūtra of Paiṭhīnasi to the effect that [[di|a]] person should marry a girl who is beyond the third degree on the mother’s side and beyond the fifth on the father’s side.1 On Yāj. III. 17 the Mit. quotes two sūtras relating to impurity on death.2 The Smṛticandrikā, [[A parārka|Aparārka]], [[Hara datta|Haradatta]] and other writers quote numerous sūtras of Paiṭhīnasi. The [[Smyticandrika|Smṛticandrikā]] has a prose quotation on the duties of women.3 In another place the Smṛticandrikā (II. p. 263) cites a sūtra on partition.4 [[Aparūrka|Aparārka]] (p. 112) quotes two verses of Paiṭhīnasi recommending the practice of sati to women of all castes except [[Brūhmana|Brāhmaṇa]] women. Aparārka (p. 239) quotes a sūtra saying that the food of astrologers;

[[P285]] bell-makers and grāmakūṭas is poison.[[978|5]] Aparārka quotes (on p. 744) a sūtra of Śaṅkha-Likhita and Paiṭhīnasi about inheritance to a deceased sonless man.[[376|6]] [[A parārka|Aparārka]] quotes another important prose passage7 “the wealth of (a heirless) [[grotriya|śrotriya]] goes to the pariṣad and not to the king, the king should not appropriate the wealth of temples and guilds, [[depo sits|deposits]] and the wealth of minors and women”. Then the sūtra quotes a verse on the same topic, which seems to refer to Manu 9. 194 in the word ‘of sixfold origin’ ([[gadāgama|ṣaḍāgama]]). Another sūtra quoted by Aparārka (p. 754) says ‘when an appointed daughter dies, her husband does not take her wealth; if she be issueless, her mother or mother-in-law should receive it’. Aparārka quotes a verse of Paiṭhīnasi (p. 921) ‘at the time of marriage, famines, sacrifices, fairs and pilgrimages there is no impurity due to birth or death’.

Fragments of [[Paithinusi|Paiṭhīnasi]] Dharmasūtra were edited by Prof. T. R. Chintamani (1939) and published in Annals of Oriental Research (University of [[Madrus|Madras]]). The Mit. on Yāj. I. 53 quotes a prose passage of P. limiting sapiṇḍa relationship to five degrees on the father’s side and to three on the mother’s side. [[Ou|On]] Yāj. I. 254 the Mit. quotes a verse about the [[sa piṇķī. karala|sapiṇḍī-karaṇa]] of a childless woman by her husband along with her mother-in-law. Mit. quotes prose passages as well as verses from Paiṭhīnasi. Vide Mit. on Yāj. I. 256, III. 28-29 for quotations in verse and for quotations in prose on Yāj. III. 17, 19, 30, 265. Kalpataru in several Kāṇḍas quotes P. mostly in prose and sometimes in verse; P. is quoted in [[Srāduha kanda|Śrāddhakāṇḍa]] 17 times, 29 times in [[Brahmucāri|Brahmacārī]], 6 times in [[Vyavahira|Vyavahāra]], 32 times in Gṛhastha. Haradatta [[ou|on]] Gaut. 14. 17 quotes a verse of P. stating that the days of [[muuruing|mourning]] to be

[[P286]] observed by a son being far away are ten from the day [[be|he]] hears of the death of his parents. P. in Vyavahāra-kalpataru p. 627 provides ‘[[womenima|women8]] are the presiding deities of the house, no rules of [[saucil|śauca]] (purification) or vrata or fasting are [[obliga tory|obligatory]] for them; they reach the highest worlds by merely [[look ing|looking]] after their husbands’. This is not to be understood literally but what it conveys is the great worth of [[pati susrisa.’|‘pati-śuśrūṣā’]]; compare Manu V. 155 and Yāj. I. 87. Kalpa° on Vyava° p. 742 quotes a verse as occurring in both Śaṅkha-likhita and Paiṭhīnasi. Viśvarūpa (on Yāj. III. 262 p. 147) provides (in prose) a prāyaścitta for a goghna (one who causes a cow’s death). [[Paithioasi|Paiṭhīnasi]] appears to have held the same view that is ascribed to Aupajaṅghani in Baud. Dh. S. II. 2. 39-41 (these verses being also quoted by Āp. Dh. II. 6. 13. 6 and also in Vas. 17. 9 without name in both). Vide H. of Dh. Vol. II. p. 602 on 1417. Kalpataru on [[Vyava hāra|Vyavahāra]] p. 603 has: पैठीनसिः । तस्माद्रक्षेद्भार्यां सर्वतः । मा स्म सङ्करो भवत्विति । आह अप्रमत्ता रक्षत तन्तुमेनं मा वः क्षेत्रे परबीजान्यवाप्सुः । भार्यां रक्षत कौमारीं बिभ्यतः पररेतसः ।. One expects वाप्सुः instead of अवाप्सुः (as मा precedes). The editor quotes only विवादरत्नाकर on p. 603 probably to clinch his thesis that Caṇḍeśvara merely borrows matters from [[Lakṣmidhura|Lakṣmīdhara]] and does not refer to Baud., Āp. or Vas., whom I had quoted years before in Vol. II (of H. of Dh.).
