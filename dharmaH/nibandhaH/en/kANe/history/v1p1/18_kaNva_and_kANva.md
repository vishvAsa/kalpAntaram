title = "18 Kaṇva and Kāṇva"

+++

From the Āp. Dh. S. it appears that Kaṇva and [[Kāụva|Kāṇva]] were two distinct writers on dharma. In I. 6. 19. 2 Āpastamba starts the question as to the persons, food at whose house was permitted. He states various views on that point and says that [[Kanya|Kaṇva]] was of opinion that food may be taken at anybody’s house provided the latter offered it with a request ( I. 6. 19. 3 ) and that the opinion of Eka, Kuṇika, Kāṇva, Kutsa and [[Puskarasāili|Puṣkarasādi]] ( [[1.|I.]] 6. 19. 7 ) was that only that food that was offered by a pure and religious man should be partaken. In another place ( [[L.|I.]] 10. 28. 1 ) [[Apastambé|Āpastamba]] gives it as the opinion of Kautsa, Hārīta, [[Kajıva|Kaṇva]] and [[Puskara sādi|Puṣkarasādi]] that a man became a thief if in any case whatever he appropriates another’s belongings. The Kumbhakoṇam edition of Pandit Halasyanatha reads Kāṇva for Kaṇva. 

Kaṇva is quoted a few times in the [[Smyticandrika|Smṛticandrikā]] on Āhnika ( daily duties ) and śrāddha. One of these quotations is in prose ( I. p. 97 ).[^252] Haradatta in his commentary on Gautama cites verses of Kaṇva in several places ( e. g. [[Guu tama Dh. S,|Gautama Dh. S.]] 21. 3, 23. 3 and 11 ).[^253] The first verse bears [[&|a]] close resemblance to Manu XI. 180 and Baud. Dh. S. II. 1. 62. Kaṇva is quoted in the Ācāramayūkha and the [[Srāddha mayūkha.|Śrāddhamayūkha.]]

The Mit. ( on Yāj. III. 58 ) quotes a verse of [[Kūṇva|Kāṇva]] on the length of the stay of a [[saimyāsin|saṃnyāsin]] in a village or town. On Yāj. [[I11.|III.]] 260 the Mit. quotes a verse of Kaṇva stating the prāyaścitta for a [[brālimana|brāhmaṇa]] having intercourse with the kṣatriya [[wise|wife]] of his teacher.

In the Madras Govt. Oriental Library there is a [[mo.|ms.]] of Kaṇva ( vol. V, p. 1929 No. 2624 ).

Maskarin on Gaut. Dh. S. frequently quotes passages from Kaṇva ( in prose and verse ) ; vide on I. 33 and 39 ( both prose ), V. 21 ( prose definition of Vedapāraga ), IX. 1 ( prose ), X. 53 ( verse on five kinds of [[brāddhas|śrāddhas]], nitya, naimittika,

[[P274]]

kāmya, vṛddhiśrāddha and pārvaṇa ), XI. 18 ( verse viz. pūrvajanmakṛtam karma daivamityabhidhīyate ), XV. 17 ( verse ), XVII. I ( verse on Strīdhana ), XV. 3 ( three verses on prāyaścitta ).
