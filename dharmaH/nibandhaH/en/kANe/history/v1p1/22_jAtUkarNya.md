Viśvarūpa ( on Yāj. I. 4-5 ) quotes a verse of [[Vrudha|Vṛddha]]-Yājñavalkya in which [[Jātukarya|Jātūkarṇya]] is mentioned as an expounder of dharma.[[389|[^262]]] The name is [[variouly|variously]] written as Jātūkarṇi or [[Jātākarnya|Jātūkarṇya]] or as [[Jatukarna|Jātukarṇa]]. The [[Smyticandrikā|Smṛticandrikā]] quotes a passage from [[Angiras|Aṅgiras]] in which Jātūkarṇya is enumerated among writers of Upasmṛtis. Viśvarūpa quotes prose passages from Jātūkarṇya a number of times.[[363|[^263]]] On Yāj. I. 1, he gives a sūtra about “ pratilomas ”; on Yāj. I. 2 there is [[&|a]] sūtra saying that a pupil should not all of a sudden put a question to his teacher in an assembly of people, on [[Yūj.|Yāj.]] I. 29 about a [[Ksatriya|Kṣatriya]] and [[Vaisya|Vaiśya]] wearing an [[Upavita|Upavīta]] of hemp and wool when initiated for sacrifice, on Yāj. I. 37 as to the age when a Brāhmaṇa became a [[vrūtya|vrātya]], about the prohibition of marrying another wife of a [[diferent|different]] caste when one has already married a savarṇa wife ( on [[Yaj.|Yāj.]] I. 79 ), about the time for [[sraddhas|śrāddhas]] ( on [[Yaj.|Yāj.]] I. 215 ). These [[quota tions|quotations]] show that Jātūkarṇya composed [[id|a]] sūtra work on ācāra and śrāddha, which was comparatively [[uncient|ancient]]. Maskarin on Gaut. Dh. S. quotes, on different sūtras, a number of verses e. g. on 10. 61 ( three verses ), 14. 31, 15. 1, 21. 3, 22. 20-21 ( two verses ), 22. 36 &c. The [[Smrti-can drikā|Smṛti-candrikā]] quotes verses of [[Jātukarıya|Jātūkarṇya]] five times on [[Ahnika|Āhnika]] and twelve times on [[Sraddha|Śrāddha]] and a prose passage ( [[Alnika|Āhnika]] p. 114, Gharpure’s ed. ) on what is meant by [[Uttariya|Uttarīya]] ( in [[Srauta|Śrauta]] or Smārta rites ). Aparārka ( p. 1069 on Yāj. III. 253 ) quotes a verse of J. that if a boy whose [[upanuyanu|upanayana]] had not been [[perfo rmed|performed]] drinks by mistake liquor the [[prayascitta|prāyaścitta]] is three [[krc chras|kṛcchras]] to be undergone by the boy’s mother, brother or father. Viśvarūpa quotes several prose passages of [[Jātākarụya|Jātūkarṇya]] ( vol. I. pp. 7, 46, 48, 144 on [[Sraddha|Śrāddha]] ), while Mit. quotes several verses on Yāj. I. 256, III. 17, 30, 253, 259-60. The name Jātūkarṇya is an ancient one. The [[Kītyāyana Srautasūtra|Kātyāyana Śrautasūtra]] ( IV. 1. 23 [[ft|ff]] )[^264] mentions the view of Jātūkarṇya on [[pinda pitryajñol|piṇḍapitṛyajña]] when all the three paternal ancestors of the performer are not [[deal|dead]] and the Kalpataru on Śrāddha ( p. 240 ) remarks ‘ [[Jivatpit;kasya trayah pakśāṇ|Jīvatpitṛkasya trayaḥ pakṣāḥ]] ’.
