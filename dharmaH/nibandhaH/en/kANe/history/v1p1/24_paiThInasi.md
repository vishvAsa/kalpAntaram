Though not enumerated in [[Yājsavalkya|Yājñavalkya]], Paiṭhīnasi seems to have been a comparatively ancient sūtrakāra on Dharma.

On Yāj. III. 262 Viśvarūpa quotes a sūtra of Paiṭhīnasi on the prāyaścitta for kiling a cow. Dr. Jolly (R. und [[$|S]]. p. 12) following Dr. Caland [[("|(']]Ahnencult &c.' pp. 99, 109) thinks that Paiṭhīnasi [[belenys|belongs]] to the Atharvaveda as the prose quotations on [[frāddha|śrāddha]] agree with the ritual of the Atharvans. The Mit. (on Yāj. I. 53) quotes a sūtra of Paiṭhīnasi to the effect that [[di|a]] person should marry a girl who is beyond the third degree on the mother's side and beyond the fifth on the father's side.[^271] On Yāj. III. 17 the Mit. quotes two sūtras relating to impurity on death.[^272] The Smṛticandrikā, [[A parārka|Aparārka]], [[Hara datta|Haradatta]] and other writers quote numerous sūtras of Paiṭhīnasi. The [[Smyticandrika|Smṛticandrikā]] has a prose quotation on the duties of women.[^273] In another place the Smṛticandrikā (II. p. 263) cites a sūtra on partition.[^274] [[Aparūrka|Aparārka]] (p. 112) quotes two verses of Paiṭhīnasi recommending the practice of sati to women of all castes except [[Brūhmana|Brāhmaṇa]] women. Fragments of [[Paithinusi|Paiṭhīnasi]] Dharmasūtra were edited by Prof. T. R. Chintamani (1939) and published in Annals of Oriental Research (University of [[Madrus|Madras]]). The Mit. on Yāj. I. 53 quotes a prose passage of P. limiting sapiṇḍa relationship to five degrees on the father's side and to three on the mother's side. [[Ou|On]] Yāj. I. 254 the Mit. quotes a verse about the [[sa piṇķī. karala|sapiṇḍī-karaṇa]] of a childless woman by her husband along with her mother-in-law. Mit. quotes prose passages as well as verses from Paiṭhīnasi. Vide Mit. on Yāj. I. 256, III. 28-29 for quotations in verse and for quotations in prose on Yāj. III. 17, 19, 30, 265. Kalpataru in several Kāṇḍas quotes P. mostly in prose and sometimes in verse; P. is quoted in [[Srāduha kanda|Śrāddhakāṇḍa]] 17 times, 29 times in [[Brahmucāri|Brahmacārī]], 6 times in [[Vyavahira|Vyavahāra]], 32 times in Gṛhastha. P. in Vyavahāra-kalpataru p. 627 provides '[[womenima|women[^277a]]] are the presiding deities of the house, no rules of [[saucil|śauca]] (purification) or vrata or fasting are [[obliga tory|obligatory]] for them; they reach the highest worlds by merely [[look ing|looking]] after their husbands'. This is not to be understood literally but what it conveys is the great worth of [[pati susrisa.'|‘pati-śuśrūṣā’]]; compare Manu V. 155 and Yāj. I. 87. Kalpa° on Vyava° p. 742 quotes a verse as occurring in both Śaṅkha-likhita and Paiṭhīnasi. Viśvarūpa (on Yāj. III. 262 p. 147) provides (in prose) a prāyaścitta for a goghna (one who causes a cow's death). [[Paithioasi|Paiṭhīnasi]] appears to have held the same view that is ascribed to Aupajaṅghani in Baud. Kalpataru on [[Vyava hāra|Vyavahāra]] p. 603 has: पैठीनसिः । तस्माद्रक्षेद्भार्यां सर्वतः । मा स्म सङ्करो भवत्विति । आह अप्रमत्ता रक्षत तन्तुमेनं मा वः क्षेत्रे परबीजान्यवाप्सुः । भार्यां रक्षत कौमारीं बिभ्यतः पररेतसः ।.
