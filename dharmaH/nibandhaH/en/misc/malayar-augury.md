Due to my grandfather having died on an inauspicious day, two Malayar (ST) priests sacrificed a rooster, and inspected its entrails to look for omens.

They told me it's called Akanāḷ Bali (Inner-Day/Asterism Sacrifice). Haruspicy in Malabar.

Death during an inauspicious day according to certain principles afflicts the dead with Akanāḷ Doṣam apparently, after the usual Antyeṣṭi rites, two Malayar-s came to my grandfather's house on the prescribed night and did a few elaborate rituals followed by the sacrifice.

One of them was a senior-citizen, the other seemed to be in his 30s, the older priest picked up the rooster by its wings, then beheaded it and set the head aside, the wings were cut as well. The body flopped around and the neck pointed to the south when it stopped.

After which he drained the blood from the body on the banana-leaf and mixed it with a number of ritual ingredients I couldn't make out in the darkness and performed more rituals while dipping the body in the mixture.

This ended the rite and then he told us that the body not spasming too much and pointing to the south was indicative of good omens for his (my grandfather) ātmāvu (self).
