## Archana combo
> My brAhmaNa colleague's relatives in Andhra, I was told, preferred paurohityaM when they have to choose between becoming an archaka in an Alaya or paurohityaM. Reason is given as more relative freedom and money. - goghRtam

> Most Brahmins (in bengal) do both. Are employed in a temple to perform the worship and also do paurohityam for yajamanas. If they have to do it for yajamanas in another place then they give the responsibility for the daily puja to another Brahmin. It may be because the temples are not Agamic mostly so not much ritual duty other than the main puja. - shardula23

> In Siṃhapurī, Śaivāgama Ācāryas do Smārta paurohityam but more importantly, they do Śaivakrama-kāmyahomas for their largely Śaiva clientele: gaṇapatihoma, skandahoma, Āyuṣahoma using amṛteśamūlamantra, navagrahahoma using śaiva mantras, etc - GA
