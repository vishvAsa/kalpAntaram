Yajñas are classified into 2 categories- śrauta with 3 fires: gārhapatya, āhavanīya and dakshińa and smārta with single aupāsanāgni. If you look at vedic literature, it's almost entirely devoted to śrauta (it's śruti afterall). Gŕhya rituals almost appears as an afterthought.

However, commonsense dictates that a single fire ritual had to have been a precursor. This is exactly what we find in tradition too.

> gandharvebhyo varaṃ labdhvā  
> tretāgniṃ samakalpayat|  
eko agniḥ pūrvamāsīd  
ailas tretām akalpayat||  
> (Hari vaṃśa 1.126.47)

Vāyu 91.48 too has this:

> Eko.agniḥ pūrvamāsīd vai  
> ailastrīṃstān akalpayat|

In tretāyuga king aila, after learning from gandharvas made the three fires. Before that there was yajña with single fire only.
