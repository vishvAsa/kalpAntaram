<details><summary>मेधातिथिः</summary>

गतार्थो ऽयम् । दुराचारो ऽप्य् अनवज्ञेय[^७७४] इत्य् अर्थः ॥ ९.३१८ ॥


[^७७४]:
     M G: avijñeya; J: anavijñeya
</details>

<details><summary>कुल्लूकः</summary>

यथाग्निर्महातेजाः श्मशाने शवं दहन्कार्येऽपि नैव दुष्टो भवति किन्तु पुनरपि यज्ञेषु हूयमानोऽभिवर्धते॥ ३१८॥
</details>

<details><summary>Bühler</summary>

318	The brilliant fire is not contaminated even in burial-places, and, when presented with oblations (of butter) at sacrifices, it again increases mightily.
</details>
