<details><summary>गङ्गानथ-मूलानुवादः</summary>

This is the rule that the righteous king shall observe, in the case of transgressions by owners, by cattle and by the keepers.—(244)
</details>

<details><summary>मेधातिथिः</summary>

सुबोधो ऽयं श्लोकः ॥ ८.२४४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>गङ्गानथ-भाष्यानुवादः</summary>

This verse is easily intelligible.—(244)
</details>

<details><summary>गङ्गानथ-टिप्पन्यः</summary>

This verse is quoted in *Vivādaratnākara* (p. 176);—and in
*Vīramitrodaya* (Vyavahāra, 139a).
</details>

<details><summary>कुल्लूकः</summary>

स्वामिनां पालानां चारक्षणादपराधे पशूनां च सस्यभक्षणरूपे व्यतिक्रमे धर्मप्रधानो भूपतिरेतत्पूर्वोकं कर्तव्यमनुतिष्ठेत् ॥२४४॥
</details>

<details><summary>गोविन्दराजः</summary>

**एतद्** इति । पशुभिः सस्यभक्षणेन स्वामिनां पालानां चापराधे देवपश्वादिसस्यभक्षणे धर्मप्रधानो राजा एतत् पूर्वोक्तं कर्तव्यम् अनुतिष्ठेत् ॥ ८.२४४ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>भारुचिः</summary>

उपसंहाराथः श्लोकः ॥ ८.२४३ ॥
</details>

<details><summary>Bühler</summary>

244	To these rules a righteous king shall keep in (all cases of) transgressions by masters, their cattle, and herdsmen.
</details>
